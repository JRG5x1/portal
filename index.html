Portal

Open BEacon.Your Jibo, in your browser.

Enter Jibo’s IP address to open BEacon, the web app for watching his screen, managing music, changing his look, and installing skills.

Your phone or computer needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as Jibo. The portal only sends you to Jibo’s own address; BEacon runs on Jibo himself.

Connect to JiboBEacon · port 8123
http://:8123

It’s on Jibo’s boot screen, or in your router’s list of devices.

Quick answers

Can’t connect?

The page won’t load after I click Access BEacon.

Make sure Jibo is on and finished booting, that your device is on the same Wi-Fi, and that the IP address is right. If his IP changed, check his boot screen or your router again.

My browser says the connection is “not secure.”

BEacon is served from Jibo over your home network with plain http://, so browsers show this notice. It’s expected for a device on your own Wi-Fi.

Does BEacon need my Jibo to be on 5x1?

Yes. BEacon comes with 5x1’s community software. If you haven’t set Jibo up yet, start with the Connect to 5x1 guide.

Does the portal store my IP address?

Recent addresses are saved only in this browser, so you can tap them next time. Clear them anytime with Forget recent addresses.
